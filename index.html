WHAT IS A HOUSE CHURCH?

A house church is a community that gathers regularly in a home to follow Jesus and share real life together. Christ City is one church family who gathers on Sundays and shares life in house churches throughout the week.

  1. 1. IN A HOME

    We gather in homes to study Scripture, share meals, pray, care for one another and love our neighbors.

  2. 2. GUIDED BY PASTORS

    Each house church has pastors who shepherd the community. Everyone has something to contribute, and as we grow we start new house churches.

  3. 3. TOGETHER ON SUNDAY

    On Sundays we come together to worship, pray, learn from Scripture and celebrate what God is doing in our city. Launch Sunday is March 7, 2027.

House churches are coming soon. Bring your questions, grab a plate and pull up a chair.

WHO WE ARE

Kenny and Kim have been going to church together since middle school. That’s also when both of them first sensed a call to ministry, and they have been following it for 26 years.

For most of that time the work was split. Kim worked in teaching and social work while Kenny served in pastoral ministry. Then they both felt a shift toward doing it together.

Christ City is the church they are planting side by side, and they will lead it as co-pastors.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

We aren’t here to fight culture wars or win arguments. We want to look at Jesus and live the way he lived, so that when you go home, what you remember is Jesus.

OUR VISION

To see our city renewed by Christ through people who live like Jesus.
