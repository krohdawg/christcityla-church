HOUSE CHURCHES

WHAT IS A HOUSE CHURCH?

A house church is a community that gathers regularly in a home to follow Jesus and share real life together.

Guided by house church pastors, its members take responsibility for growing in their faith, making disciples together, and joining Jesus in pursuing people who are far from God.

We gather in homes to study Scripture, share meals, pray, care for one another, and love our neighbors. These everyday practices help us center our lives on Christ and put our faith into action.

Everyone has something to contribute. Together, we help one another take steps of faith, build relationships with people who don’t yet know Jesus, and make room for others to belong. As communities grow and new leaders develop, we work toward starting new house churches so more people can experience life with Christ.

You’re welcome to participate even if you’re still exploring what you believe. Bring your questions, grab a plate, and pull up a chair.

A CHURCH OF HOUSE CHURCHES

Christ City is one church family who gathers on Sundays and shares life in house churches throughout the week. We are united as one by aligning to the vision, mission, and values overseen by our church-wide leadership.

On Sundays, we come together to worship, pray, learn from Scripture, and celebrate what God is doing throughout our city. In house churches, we live out that faith through deeper relationships, shared responsibility, and a commitment to making disciples.

Our hope is to see communities of followers of Jesus taking root wherever our people live, centering their lives on Christ and pursuing people with love.
