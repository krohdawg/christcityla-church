What Is a House Church? A Simple Guide

A house church is a community that gathers regularly in a home to follow Jesus and share real life together. At Christ City Church in La Mirada, house churches are a big part of who we are.

What happens in a house church

We gather in homes to study Scripture, share meals, pray, care for one another, and love our neighbors. These everyday practices help us center our lives on Christ and put our faith into action.

Who leads it

Each house church is guided by house church pastors who shepherd the community. Its members take responsibility for growing in their faith, making disciples together, and joining Jesus in pursuing people who are far from God. Everyone has something to contribute.

How it fits with Sunday

Christ City is one church family who gathers on Sundays and shares life in house churches throughout the week. On Sundays we come together to worship, pray, learn from Scripture, and celebrate what God is doing throughout our city. In house churches we live out that faith through deeper relationships and shared responsibility.

What if I am still exploring?

You are welcome to participate even if you are still exploring what you believe. Bring your questions, grab a plate, and pull up a chair.

How new house churches start

As communities grow and new leaders develop, we work toward starting new house churches so more people can experience life with Christ. The first ones are coming soon in La Mirada, Norwalk, Whittier and Bellflower. Live somewhere else? Yours could be next.
