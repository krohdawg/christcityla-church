Looking for a Church in La Mirada? Meet Christ City Church

If you are looking for a church in La Mirada, Christ City Church is a new one taking shape right now. Launch Sunday is March 7, 2027, and there are ways to meet us before then.

Who we are

Christ City is a new church helping people center their lives on Jesus and join Him in pursuing people with love. Whether you are exploring faith, returning to church, or looking for a church community, you are invited to come curious.

The church is led by co-pastors Kenny and Kim Baker. They have been going to church together since middle school and have followed a call to ministry for 26 years. Christ City is the church they are planting side by side in La Mirada. Read their story.

Where we meet

Sunday gatherings meet at Heights Christian Schools, Bluefield Campus, 12900 Bluefield Ave, La Mirada, CA 90638. Weekly Sunday gatherings begin on Launch Sunday, March 7, 2027.

A church of house churches

Christ City is one church family who gathers on Sundays and shares life in house churches throughout the week. In a house church we study Scripture, share meals, pray, care for one another and love our neighbors. See how house churches work.

What we believe

We are affiliated with the Evangelical Covenant Church. In our own words, we want to look at Jesus and live the way he lived, so that when you go home, what you remember is Jesus. You do not need to have it figured out to find belonging here. Read what we believe.

Ways to meet us before launch

  • Christmas at Christ City, Sunday, December 20, 4 to 7 PM
  • Discover Christ City, four Sundays starting January 10
  • A public preview service on February 7

Every date is on our Coming up page. Questions? Text us at 562-724-4465.
