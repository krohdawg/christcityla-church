Christmas Services in La Mirada: Christmas at Christ City, December 20

Looking for a Christmas service in La Mirada this year? Christ City Church invites you to Christmas at Christ City on Sunday, December 20, 2026, from 4 to 7 PM.

Celebrate Jesus’ birth with us through worship and a hopeful message. It is our first preview service, and families, singles, couples and friends of all ages are welcome.

The night

  • 4 PM, family photo scavenger hunt. It starts from your own home. Bring a phone or camera.
  • 5 PM, potluck, photo sharing and prizes at the Bluefield Campus.
  • 6 PM, Christmas preview service.

Where

Heights Christian Schools, Bluefield Campus, 12900 Bluefield Ave, La Mirada, CA 90638.

How to come

Registration is free. Sign up once for your group on our Christmas page, and we will see you on the 20th.

New to church?

You do not need to have it figured out to find belonging here. Whether you are exploring faith, returning to church, or looking for a church community, you are invited to come curious.

Questions? Text us at 562-724-4465.
